Gomi Navi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Gomi Navi ("the app") is a free, unofficial garbage-collection calendar and sorting guide for Kutchan and Niseko, Hokkaido, Japan. It is made by an individual developer (amirudev) and is not operated by any town office.

Summary

The app does not have accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data.

Separately, the project website (gomi-navi.amiru.dev) has a sign-up form for people who want to join the closed test of the app. That form does collect an email address and a WhatsApp number or LINE ID; see "Closed-test sign-up (website only)" below.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen language, town, zone/neighbourhood and reminder settings are stored locally on your phone (app preferences). They are never uploaded.
  • Garbage-day reminders are scheduled as local notifications by your device.

Permissions

  • Location (optional, only while the app is open): used when you tap "Suggest my zone" to work out which collection zone you are in. The calculation happens entirely on your device; your location is not sent to us or anyone else. You can use the app without granting it.
  • Notifications: to show garbage-day reminders. You can disable them at any time.
  • Internet: to download map tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) and to open official town web pages in your browser. Like any web request, these services can see your IP address; see the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. We do not receive this information.
  • Boot completed: to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.

Closed-test sign-up (website only)

This section applies only to the optional sign-up form on gomi-navi.amiru.dev. It does not apply to the app itself, which collects nothing.

  • What we collect: the email address of your Google account, your WhatsApp number or LINE ID (whichever you choose), the language of the page you used, and the time you submitted the form. You must tick a consent box before sending.
  • Why: only to add you as a tester in Google Play Console and to contact you on WhatsApp or LINE about it.
  • Where it is stored: in a private storage bucket (Vercel Blob) that only the developer can read. When someone signs up, the developer receives a push notification through ntfy.sh that says someone signed up; it contains no personal data.
  • Sharing: your details are not sold or shared with anyone else. The email address is entered into Google Play Console, which is Google's service, so that you can become a tester.
  • Spam protection and consent record: to limit abuse, the server keeps a short-lived counter per anonymised (salted hash) IP address for up to three hours; it is not used to identify you. The sign-up record also stores the time you gave consent and the version of this policy.
  • Retention and deletion: kept while the closed test is running and deleted afterwards. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time by contacting the developer (see Contact).
  • Website basics: the site uses no advertising or analytics trackers. It stores your language and light/dark choice in your browser (local storage) only. Like any website, the hosting provider (Vercel) processes technical request data such as your IP address; see Vercel's privacy policy.

Children

The app and the sign-up form are not directed at children. The app collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
