Gomi Navi – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Gomi Navi ("the app") is a free, unofficial garbage-collection calendar and sorting guide for Kutchan and Niseko, Hokkaido, Japan. It is made by an individual developer (amirudev) and is not operated by any town office.

Summary

The app does not have accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

  • Your chosen language, town, zone/neighbourhood and reminder settings are stored locally on your phone (app preferences). They are never uploaded.
  • Garbage-day reminders are scheduled as local notifications by your device.

Permissions

  • Location (optional, only while the app is open): used when you tap "Suggest my zone" to work out which collection zone you are in. The calculation happens entirely on your device; your location is not sent to us or anyone else. You can use the app without granting it.
  • Notifications: to show garbage-day reminders. You can disable them at any time.
  • Internet: to download map tiles from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org) and to open official town web pages in your browser. Like any web request, these services can see your IP address; see the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy. We do not receive this information.
  • Boot completed: to restore scheduled reminders after your phone restarts.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
